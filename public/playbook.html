The Methodologies

● ICT (Inner Circle Trader)

institutional concepts
  • What it finds: Fair Value Gaps (price left a hole), Order Blocks (the last candle before a big move), liquidity sweeps (fake breakouts that grab stops), killzones (best trading hours).
  • When it shines: Trending days with clear sessions — London and New York killzones give the cleanest ICT setups.
  • Weakness: In slow, choppy markets it draws many gaps and blocks that never get used. Needs real momentum.

● SMC (Smart Money Concepts)

institutional concepts
  • What it finds: Market structure breaks (BOS), trend changes (CHoCH), order blocks and liquidity pools — where big money likely acts.
  • When it shines: After a sweep of old highs/lows, when structure flips and price returns to an order block.
  • Weakness: Structure looks obvious only in hindsight; live, every small break can fake you out. Wait for the retest.

● VSA (Volume Spread Analysis)

volume reading
  • What it finds: What volume is telling you — buying/selling climaxes, no-demand / no-supply bars, stopping volume (smart money absorbing).
  • When it shines: At tops and bottoms. A climax bar with huge volume often marks the exact turning point.
  • Weakness: Needs real volume data. On forex (no central volume) it uses tick volume — useful, but weaker than crypto/futures.

● Price Action

pure chart reading
  • What it finds: Support/resistance zones where price reacted many times, plus engulfing candles and pin bars at those zones.
  • When it shines: Always — it is the foundation. Every other methodology works better on top of clean S/R.
  • Weakness: Alone it has no timing edge; a zone can hold five times and break on the sixth. Needs a trigger.

● Elliott Wave

wave structure
  • What it finds: 5-wave impulses and A-B-C corrections, labeled by strict rules (wave 2 never retraces 100%, wave 3 is never the shortest).
  • When it shines: Strong trends — catching wave 3 or the end of wave 5 is the highest-reward trade in technical analysis.
  • Weakness: Our labels are a rules-based approximation, not a human analyst. Counts can redraw as new candles form. Use as a map, not gospel.

● Chart Patterns

classic patterns
  • What it finds: Head & Shoulders, Double Tops/Bottoms, wedges, flags — with neckline breaks confirmed.
  • When it shines: Reversal patterns at big S/R zones, continuation flags inside strong trends.
  • Weakness: Patterns fail often without volume or structure confirmation. A lone pattern is a coin flip.

● Trendlines + S/R

dynamic levels
  • What it finds: Auto-drawn trendlines (3+ touches) and horizontal zones, with breakout and retest/bounce signals.
  • When it shines: Clean trending markets — the third touch of a trendline is one of the most reliable entries in trading.
  • Weakness: In chop, trendlines break constantly. More touches = stronger line; two touches is just a guess.

● Sentiment & News

fundamental + crowd
  • What it finds: Upcoming high-impact news (NFP, CPI, FOMC) drawn on the chart, live retail long/short %, Fear & Greed, and a % bias per event.
  • When it shines: Before big news — knowing retail is 80% long into CPI keeps you from joining the crowd right before the trap.
  • Weakness: Bias % is a model estimate, not a prediction. News can surprise; spreads widen; slippage is real.

● SMT Divergence

intermarket ICT
  • What it finds: When your asset makes a higher high but the correlated reference asset fails to confirm (or vice versa) — smart-money disagreement.
  • When it shines: At major tops/bottoms, especially XAUUSD vs DXY-style pairs. Divergence often appears days before the turn.
  • Weakness: Correlations break during risk-on/risk-off shocks. Divergence can persist longer than your patience.

● Displacement Radar

impulse scoring
  • What it finds: Scores every strong leg by body %, volume and speed — real institutional displacement glows, weak noise fades.
  • When it shines: Filtering: only trade order blocks and FVGs that were born from a glowing (high-score) leg.
  • Weakness: A high score says "big money moved", not "price will continue". News spikes score high and reverse fast.

● Sweep Magnets

liquidity targets
  • What it finds: Pools of resting liquidity (equal highs/lows, old extremes) ranked by magnet strength — where price is likely drawn.
  • When it shines: Setting targets: aim where the liquidity sits, not at round numbers. Strongest use is with a sweep entry.
  • Weakness: Price does not have to visit every pool. Magnets show attraction, not a schedule.

● Killzone Sessions

time filter
  • What it finds: Asian / London / New York session boxes and the true midnight open line — when, not just where.
  • When it shines: As a filter for everything else: ICT and VSA setups inside killzones win far more than the same setups at dead hours.
  • Weakness: It is a filter, not a signal. Session alone tells you nothing about direction.

🤝 Synergy Recipes — combinations that work

1. The Classic Trio: Sweep + FVG + VSA no-supply

Price sweeps sell-side liquidity, leaves a bullish FVG, and volume shows no supply on the pullback.

Why it works: the trap (sweep) + the footprint (FVG) + the confirmation (no sellers left). Three independent witnesses.

2. VSA stopping volume + ICT bullish OB at London killzone

Stopping volume appears exactly at a bullish order block during London hours.

Why it works: smart-money absorption + institutional zone + prime time. Time, place and footprint align.

3. SMT divergence + sweep = trap confirmation

XAUUSD sweeps a high but the reference asset refuses to confirm — divergence tags print at the sweep.

Why it works: one market's lie is exposed by the other market's honesty. The best fakeouts show divergence first.

4. Trendline break + VSA climax filter

Only take trendline breaks when volume does NOT show a climax; skip the break if a buying climax prints into it.

Why it works: most failed breakouts are climax exhaustion in disguise. VSA tells the real break from the trap.

5. Sweep magnets as targets, sweep entry as trigger

Enter on a liquidity sweep, aim at the strongest magnet (🧲) in the trade direction.

Why it works: you enter where stops were hunted and exit where the next hunt will happen — trading the full liquidity cycle.

6. Displacement filter for ICT: only glowing FVGs

Take only FVGs and order blocks created by a high displacement score (⚡ glowing leg).

Why it works: weak legs leave weak footprints. Real displacement means real money behind the zone.

7. News zones + sentiment badge = stay out of the crowd

When a tier-1 news zone approaches and retail is 75%+ long, stand aside or fade the crowd — don't chase.

Why it works: crowded positioning into news is how stop-hunts are funded. The badge shows you the crowd in real time.

8. Double top + SMT divergence + killzone

A double top forms at a magnet level, SMT prints divergence on the second top, all inside a killzone.

Why it works: pattern + intermarket + time — the highest-conviction short setup in the ICT playbook.

⚠️ Honest note: No combination guarantees wins. Synergies raise probability — they do not remove risk. The Live Estimator on the terminal shows the modeled trade frequency and win rate for your current combination. Always use a stop loss. Not financial advice.
